How to use WordReff
Quick Start
1
Search a paper on Google Scholar or another academic source.
2
Click Cite → choose BibTeX.
3
Paste the BibTeX entry into WordReff.
4
Click Add to save it, or Add & Cite to instantly insert the citation into Word.
Library & Citations
  • Use the Search bar to quickly find saved references.
  • Click Cite beside a saved entry to reuse it anywhere in the document.
  • Select multiple citations and use Merge citations for grouped references.
  • Use Unmerge citations to separate grouped citations again.
Bibliography & Styles
  • Change citation styles anytime using the style dropdown.
  • Supported styles include APA, IEEE, Harvard, ACS, and Vancouver.
  • Click Update bibliography to refresh all citations and references.
  • Customize bibliography font, size, spacing, alignment, and color.
💡 WordReff automatically keeps citation numbering and bibliography ordering synchronized across your document.
Smart citation management directly inside Microsoft Word
WordReff lets you paste BibTeX from Google Scholar, store references, insert citations, merge citations, and automatically generate formatted bibliographies without leaving Word.

2. Paste it into WordReff
3. Click Add & Cite to insert citations instantly

WordReff Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

WordReff is a Microsoft Word add-in that helps users insert citations and build bibliographies from BibTeX entries.

Data Collection

WordReff does not collect any personal data, user identifiers, document contents, analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics.

All data (BibTeX entries, citations, bibliography) remains inside the user’s Word document. No data is transmitted to any external server.

Third-Party Services

WordReff does not connect to any third-party service and does not send any data to external APIs.

App Name

This privacy policy applies to the Word add-in WordReff.
